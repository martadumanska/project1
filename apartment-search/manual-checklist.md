# Manual checklist (things automation can't do for you)

The weekly run covers what's searchable. The steps below fill the gaps. Most take 10 minutes once.

## 1. Set instant alerts (do this first: good listings go within a day)
- [ ] **Qasa**: create a profile and save searches with notifications on:
  Solna kommun, Sundbyberg, Vasastan/Norrmalm, Kungsholmen and Sollentuna. Max rent 12,000; move-in by 31 Jan 2027.
  A complete profile with a photo, a short bio, employer or university and references gets far more replies.
- [ ] **Samtrygg**: set up a "bevakning" with the same areas and budget.
- [ ] **Blocket Bostad**: save a search with notifications (Blocket blocks automated search).
- [ ] **HomeQ**: create a profile. First-hand apartments appear there without a queue, if you meet the income requirement.
- [ ] **Bostadsförmedlingen** (bostad.stockholm.se): register if you haven't. Also watch the short-term contracts.
- [ ] **KI Housing**: if you are a KI student, researcher or guest researcher, ask KI Housing about January 2027 availability now.
- [ ] **KI staff portal "Housing for rent"**: check it weekly (also covered by the automated run).

## 2. Facebook groups
Groups are private, so the automated run can't read them. Search Facebook for these terms and join the groups that have **many posts from the last few days**:

- `Lägenheter i Stockholm` / `Bostad Stockholm` / `Andrahand Stockholm`
- `Bostad Solna` / `Lägenhet Solna Sundbyberg` / `Hyra lägenhet Sollentuna`
- `Housing in Stockholm` / `Stockholm accommodation` / `Expats in Stockholm housing`
- `KI students housing` / `Karolinska Institutet housing`
- `Uppsala housing` (some people move between Uppsala and Stockholm)
- A group for people from your home country in Stockholm: these are smaller, so you have better odds.

Then:
- [ ] Turn on notifications for **all posts** in the 3–4 most active groups.
- [ ] Also check **Facebook Marketplace → Property rentals**, filtered to Stockholm and ≤ 12,000 SEK.
- [ ] Post a "looking for" ad (templates below) and repost it every 1–2 weeks.

## 3. "Looking for" post templates
Fill in the `[brackets]`. Don't post your personal number or ID.

**Svenska**
> Söker lägenhet (eller rum) i Solna / Vasastan / Sundbyberg / Sollentuna från [10–31] januari 2027, gärna [X månader / tillsvidare].
> Budget upp till 12 000 kr/mån. Jag är [ålder], [doktorand/forskare/anställd] på Karolinska Institutet och pendlar även till Uppsala, så nära pendeltåg är ett plus.
> Skötsam, rökfri, inga husdjur. Referenser från tidigare hyresvärdar finns. Hör gärna av dig i DM!

**English**
> Looking for an apartment (or room) in Solna / Vasastan / Sundbyberg / Sollentuna from [10–31] January 2027, ideally for [X months / long-term].
> Budget up to 12,000 SEK/month. I'm a [PhD student / researcher / employee] at Karolinska Institutet who also commutes to Uppsala, so being near the commuter train is a plus.
> Tidy, non-smoker, no pets, with references from previous landlords. Please DM me!

## 4. Before you pay anything
- [ ] View the place in person or on a live video call where the person shows it in real time.
- [ ] See the landlord's ID and proof they own or rent the place (BRF statement, contract).
- [ ] For a sublet: get written approval from the BRF or landlord (subletting without it is illegal, and you can be evicted).
- [ ] Sign a written contract (Qasa and Samtrygg provide one). The rent must be reasonable; you can challenge overcharging at Hyresnämnden.
- [ ] Never pay the deposit by Western Union, crypto or gift cards, or to a foreign account.
