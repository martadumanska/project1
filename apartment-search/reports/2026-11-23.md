## Scam and risk flags
- **Large deposits before a viewing** (like the 12,000 asked for Mjölnerbacken) are a common scam pattern. Only pay after a viewing and a signed contract, ideally through Qasa's or Samtrygg's own payment flow.
- **Sublets:** ask to see the BRF's or landlord's written approval. Rent above the owner's costs plus a reasonable surcharge (≈ 15% if furnished) can be challenged at Hyresnämnden.
- Blocket blocks automated search, so check it manually. Its rentals mostly come from Qasa, which was covered.
