## Scam and risk flags
- **Karolinska Vägen 1A** (Bofrid) and **Humblegatan 17** (Samtrygg): the prices are far below market and the snapshots look old. Treat them as unverified until you see the live page.
- **Samtrygg mirror domains** (`ww.`, `ww-w.`, `email.`, `kvalster.` subdomains) appear in search results. Open listings only on **www.samtrygg.se**.
- **Swap-only listings (bytesbostad)** need you to already have a rental to swap. They're now dropped automatically (see `criteria.md`).
- A landlord "working abroad" is only OK if all payment goes through Qasa's or Samtrygg's own flow. Never pay a deposit before a viewing and a signed contract, and pay deposits only to a Swedish bank account.
