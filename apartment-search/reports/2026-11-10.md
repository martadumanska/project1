## About the links
- **Listing** opens the ad itself.
- **List: find "Street"** means the site's own page for that ad isn't in the search index (true for most Qasa ads).
  The link opens the site's list for that area. Use **Find on page** (Ctrl+F / ⌘F, or "Find in page" on a phone) to search for the street name.
  If it isn't on page 1, check page 2. If it's not there at all, it has most likely been rented already.
