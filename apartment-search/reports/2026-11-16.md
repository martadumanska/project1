## Scam and risk flags
- **Stationsgatan 1 and other aggregator ads:** confirm the landlord's identity and that it's a real first-hand contract before sharing documents. Never pay anything before signing a contract.
- **Sublet rents:** charging more than the owner's own costs plus a reasonable surcharge is illegal, and you can challenge it at Hyresnämnden.
- Blocket blocks automated search, so check it manually. Its rentals mostly come from Qasa, which was covered.
