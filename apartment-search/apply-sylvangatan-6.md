## Step 1: Get set up (do this today)
- [ ] **Swedish BankID** on your phone. Qasa needs it to apply to first-hand ads from professional landlords.
  *No Swedish BankID yet?* Qasa's help says you can apply through **homeq.se** instead, since the same first-hand ads are listed there. HomeQ's own pages say membership needs Mobile BankID, so check this first.
- [ ] **Log in to Qasa** and fill in your profile completely: photo, a short "about me", and your occupation.
- [ ] **Link your Qasa account to HomeQ** (Profile → Linked accounts). You can't apply without this, and it's what starts your queue points (1 point per day).
  If your Qasa account is older, you get 1 point per day of membership, up to 500, when you link it.

## Step 2: Check that you meet the criteria
- [ ] **Income:** landlords usually ask for **2–3 × the monthly rent**. Example: at 9,000 SEK rent you'd need about 18,000–27,000 SEK/month.
  Some landlords only accept permanent employment, so check what Birger Bostad accepts (see the questions at the end).
- [ ] **Credit history:** no payment remarks (betalningsanmärkningar) or debts with the Enforcement Authority (Kronofogden).
- [ ] **Household:** for a 1-room flat this usually means 1–2 people.
- [ ] **Number of moves:** some landlords limit how many times you've moved in recent years.
- The exact requirements are blurred unless you pay for **Qasa Premium**. You don't need Premium to apply.
  Premium shows your place in the queue and the exact requirements, and lets you have 10 active applications instead of 5. That's optional and only worth it if you apply to many first-hand flats.

## Step 3: Prepare your documents (about 30 minutes)
- [ ] **ID:** passport, Swedish ID card or residence permit card.
- [ ] **Proof of income:** an employment certificate (anställningsbevis) or contract plus your latest 3 payslips.
  If you're on a PhD or research stipend, use the stipend letter. Alternatively, Qasa can estimate your income through your bank (via Tink) during the application.
- [ ] **A short personal letter** (see the template below).
- [ ] Optional: a reference from a previous landlord.

## Step 4: Apply
1. Open https://qasa.com/se/sv/home/1501017 and press **"Ansök" / "Apply"**.
2. Fill in, in this order: contact details → **ID verification with BankID** → household members → current housing → **income** (upload the documents or use the Tink estimate) → **credit check** (you approve it) → personal introduction / letter.
3. Submit, then check that the ad shows as applied in your Qasa account.

**What "vägledande efter köpoäng" means for you:** the landlord ranks applicants by queue points, but **doesn't have to pick the person with the most points**. They can also weigh your personal letter, income and household.
With few points, a **complete, well-written application** is how you stand out, so don't skip the letter. Apply **as early as you can**, before the last application date.

## Step 5: After you apply
- [ ] Turn on Qasa notifications, and check email and the Qasa app **every day**. Invitations to viewings and offers come there.
- [ ] **Viewing (visning):** go in person and bring your questions (list below).
- [ ] **Offer (erbjudande):** answer quickly; offers usually have a deadline.
  Accepting and signing a contract **resets your queue points to zero**, so only accept a place you actually want.
- [ ] Keep applying elsewhere at the same time. You can have **5 active applications**.

## Step 6: When you get the contract
- [ ] Sign the contract (normally digitally with BankID).
- [ ] **Home insurance (hemförsäkring):** most landlords require it.
- [ ] **Electricity contract (elavtal):** electricity isn't included, so sign up with a supplier from your move-in date. Ask Birger Bostad who the grid company (nätägare) is.
- [ ] Ask how **hot water** is billed (usually metered and added to the rent invoice).
- [ ] **Register your new address** with Skatteverket (anmäl flytt) **within one week of moving in**. A first-hand contract lets you do this, unlike most sublets.
- [ ] Internet: ask whether the building has broadband (bredband) installed.

---

## Questions to ask Birger Bostad (at the viewing or by message)
1. What are the exact income requirements? Is it gross income, and do you accept fixed-term employment or a stipend?
2. Is the move-in date fixed, or could it be earlier or later?
3. Is the contract open-ended (tillsvidare)? What is the notice period (usually 3 months)?
4. Roughly how much do electricity and hot water cost per month for this flat?
5. Is internet included or available? Who is the electricity grid company?
6. Is there a deposit?

## Personal letter templates
Replace the `[brackets]`. Keep it short: 4–6 sentences.

**Svenska**
> Hej! Jag heter [namn] och är [yrke, t.ex. doktorand/forskare] på [Karolinska Institutet / arbetsgivare] med en [fast/tidsbegränsad] anställning och en månadsinkomst på [belopp] kr.
> Jag söker ett långsiktigt boende i Solna nära jobbet och Solna station. Jag bor ensam, röker inte och har inga husdjur.
> Jag är noggrann och skötsam och betalar alltid i tid. Referenser från tidigare hyresvärd finns på begäran. Tack för att ni läser min ansökan!

**English**
> Hi! My name is [name] and I work as a [PhD student / researcher / job] at [Karolinska Institutet / employer] on a [permanent/fixed-term] contract, with a monthly income of [amount] SEK.
> I'm looking for a long-term home in Solna, close to work and Solna station. I live alone, don't smoke and have no pets.
> I'm tidy and reliable and always pay on time. References from a previous landlord are available on request. Thank you for considering my application!
