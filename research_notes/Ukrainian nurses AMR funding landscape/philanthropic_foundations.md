## Q1. Large global-health / AMR philanthropies (Wellcome, Novo Nordisk Foundation, Gates, Rockefeller, Mérieux, Botnar, OSF): what is open, what recurs, and can a Swedish university or Ukrainian NGO apply?

### Takeaway
- **Wellcome is effectively closed to this consortium.** From **29 October 2026**, its Discovery Research schemes (Discovery, Early-Career and Career Development Awards) accept lead applicants only from the UK and from LMICs in Africa, South Asia and South-East Asia. Neither Sweden nor Ukraine qualifies to lead. Its humanitarian-health vehicle, R2HC, is listed as closed.
- **The Novo Nordisk Foundation (NNF) is the most relevant large philanthropy.** Its *"Informing humanitarian response through research, learning and innovation"* call explicitly lists **Ukraine** as a target geography and offers grants of DKK 1–5 million for up to 2 years. However, the main applicant must be an *international humanitarian organisation*, and no 2026 round was found. NNF's AMR calls (Infectious Diseases Catalyst Grants; Challenge Programme 2027 on AMR diagnostics) are both closed and product- or tool-oriented, so they fit poorly.

### Inferences
- **Wellcome: low fit; do not plan around it.**
  - After 29 Oct 2026, neither Uppsala/KI nor any Ukrainian partner can lead a Discovery Research application, so Discovery, Early-Career and Career Development Awards are out.
  - The regional wording ("Africa, South Asia and South-East Asia") excludes Ukraine. This is an inference from the wording; Wellcome does not state it explicitly for Ukraine.
  - Swedish or Ukrainian researchers could only be *collaborators* on UK- or eligible-LMIC-led grants. That is not a realistic route for this project.
- **NNF humanitarian call: medium fit, and the best large-philanthropy option, if it recurs.**
  - *Fit.* Ukraine is in scope. The grant size (DKK 1–5 million, about EUR 134k–670k over up to 2 years) would cover a substantial component, such as nurse-led IPC improvement projects in Ternopil or other hospitals with an implementation-research layer.
  - *Lead applicant.* The lead would have to be an international humanitarian organisation. ZDOROVI qualifies only if it operates in more than one country. Otherwise an international humanitarian NGO active in Ukraine would need to lead, with Uppsala/KI and the Ukrainian partners as co-applicants.
  - *Framing.* The health sub-themes (cardiometabolic disease, climate-resilient health systems) do not name AMR/IPC. The pitch would need a health-systems-resilience framing, for example that IPC and AMR are core to a climate- and war-resilient hospital system.
  - *Timing.* If the cycle repeats (open January, close mid-April, decision September), a 2027 round would fit a start in late 2027 or 2028. That suits the follow-on or a complementary component better than a January 2027 start.
  - *Next step.* Contact NNF's humanitarian team now to ask whether a 2026 or 2027 round is planned.
- **NNF AMR calls: low fit for a nursing/implementation project.** Both the Catalyst Grants and the Challenge Programme target tools and diagnostics. Themes change yearly, so watch the 2027 Catalyst theme; a stewardship or IPC theme would change the picture.
- **Gates, Rockefeller, Fondation Mérieux, Fondation Botnar and OSF: low fit.** None showed a Ukraine-oriented AMR/IPC or nursing call in the material found (see Gaps).

## Q2. Swedish and Nordic private foundations: which can a Swedish university (Uppsala/KI) use, and when?

### Takeaway
- **No Swedish private foundation offers a call that matches this project's scale and theme as of 28 Oct 2026.**
- **Riksbankens Jubileumsfond (RJ)** is the most credible Swedish private-foundation route, for a social-science framing (nursing workforce, gender, policy uptake). Its RJ Projects call has a January window (7–27 January in 2026), and an RJ Projects 2027 round is confirmed to exist.
- **The Swedish Society of Nursing (Svensk sjuksköterskeförening)** offers small individual grants for member nurses. The 2026 deadline (15 Oct 2026) has just passed.
- **Vårdalstiftelsen** no longer exists. Most other foundations named in the brief are out of scope (Kempe, Crafoord, Carlsberg, Lundbeck) or work by nomination (Wallenberg).

### Inferences

A component on nursing leadership, gender and the health workforce, the evidence-to-policy process in AMR governance, or the history and policy of IPC in wartime Ukraine could be eligible if framed as social science (health policy, sociology of professions, gender studies).

## Q3. Nursing-specific philanthropic funders (Burdett, Florence Nightingale Foundation, ICN/FNIF, Sigma, Nursing Now, Health Foundation, J&J Foundation): which are open, and which should be approached?

### Takeaway
- **The Burdett Trust for Nursing is the highest-value relationship target among nursing-specific funders.** It has already funded a programme "strengthening palliative care nursing across Ukraine". In February 2026 it adopted a new five-year strategy with three priorities and a *"Nursing Futures"* grants programme still in development. There is no open call now, so this is an **approach-by-relationship** funder.
- **Other nursing funders give small, mostly US- or UK-tied grants.** Sigma research grants run up to about USD 5,000, with some deadlines projected for April 2027.

### Cited Findings
