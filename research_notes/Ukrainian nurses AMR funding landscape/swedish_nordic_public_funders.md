### Inferences
- The concept note's budget (SEK 13.9M, just under the SEK 14M ceiling), duration (24 months, the maximum) and start date (Jan 2027) show it targets this Sida call. If it was submitted by 16 Aug 2026, the applicants should now be waiting for, or preparing, an invited full proposal. Sida runs that stage directly with invited applicants, and no public deadline exists.
- If it was **not** submitted, there is no open Sida window. A repeat call in 2027 is uncertain for three reasons: the research cooperation appropriation fell to SEK 246M; the Ukraine strategy ends 31 Dec 2027, with a successor strategy due; and government formation is unresolved after the September 2026 election. This is speculation; no policy announcement was found.
- Ukraine's inclusion alongside LMICs in a *research* call is notable. It suggests the research strategy (2022–2028) and the Ukraine strategy are being combined. That makes the reform and EU-accession framing of the Ukraine strategy (health-system reform, workforce, AMR as an EU acquis area) a strong angle for the full proposal.

### Cited Findings
- **SIUCP 2026 call.** Opened 5 Dec 2025, closed 2 Mar 2026 23:59 CET. Themes were innovation ecosystems; transparency, accountability and anti-corruption; and gender equality, with projects to support Ukraine's EU accession and integration.
