# EU-level funding instruments for a Swedish–Ukrainian nurses' AMR/IPC research and capacity-building project (status as of 28 October 2026)

> **Verification caveat for the report writer.** In this session, direct fetching of official EU pages (ec.europa.eu Funding & Tenders Portal, health.ec.europa.eu, onehealthamr.eu, zonmw.nl) was blocked by the network policy, so no deadline was read directly off a Funding & Tenders Portal topic page. The session-wide web-search limit was also reached after about 30 queries. Every finding below comes from search-engine summaries of official pages (EU agencies, national funders and NCPs) or secondary sources (consultancies, university research offices), and the cited URL is the page the summary came from. Items marked **UNVERIFIED** come from the researcher's background knowledge and were not confirmed in this session. Re-check all deadlines on the Funding & Tenders Portal before relying on them.
>
> **Project fit yardstick used for "Fit".** HIGH = topic or instrument purpose directly covers nurse-led IPC/AMR education, implementation research, curricula or nursing research capacity, with Ukrainian eligibility. MEDIUM = covers one workstream (e.g., doctoral training, networking, staff mobility, curricula) or needs reframing. LOW = mismatched scientific scope (e.g., drug discovery) or not an open grant route.

## 1. Horizon Europe Cluster 1 (Health) 2026–2027 and Ukraine-specific Horizon Europe actions

### Takeaway
None of the Cluster 1 Health 2026–2027 topics I found covers health workforce or nursing, IPC implementation, health in war, or Ukraine's health system. The only AMR topic is a drug-development RIA (HORIZON-HLTH-2027-01-DISEASE-08, innovative antimicrobials), which is a poor fit. The 2027 Health calls appear to open on 29 Oct 2026 (tomorrow) with a 17 Feb 2027 deadline. An older version of the work programme gave 10 Feb–13 Apr 2027, so this needs checking. Ukraine is fully associated and can join almost every topic, but Cluster 1 is a low-fit route for this project.

### Inferences
- **Timing mismatch:** a Horizon Europe Health topic with a Feb 2027 deadline would not start before about Q4 2027 (roughly 5 months evaluation plus 3 months grant preparation). It cannot fund a project starting January 2027, and it fits only a scaled-up follow-on.
- The 2027 Cluster 1 calls are the last Health calls under Horizon Europe. FP10 calls are not expected before 2028 (see section 7). If the consortium wants a Horizon-scale health-systems or workforce RIA, the only window is HORIZON-HLTH-2027-01/-02/-03, and none of the topics found matches nursing/IPC. A health-systems topic would have to be reframed, at a low probability of fit.
- Cluster 1 RIA rules (UNVERIFIED in this session) require at least 3 independent legal entities from 3 different EU Member States or Associated Countries, with at least one in a Member State. Sweden plus Ukraine would therefore need a third EU or associated country partner.

## 2. European Partnership on One Health AMR (EUP OHAMR) and ERA4Health

### Takeaway
OHAMR, the successor to JPIAMR, ran its first joint transnational call (JTC 2026, treatments and adherence) with deadlines of 2 Feb and 17 Jun 2026. Funding decisions came in Oct 2026 and projects start Dec 2026–Apr 2027. **JTC 2027, "Understand, Model and Stop AMR Transmission", was pre-announced on 19 Oct 2026.** It opens 18 Nov 2026, pre-proposals are due **1 Feb 2027**, the budget is €28m and 33 funders from 26 countries take part. Its three topics (transmission modelling, decolonisation, environmental hotspots) fit healthcare IPC only partly. The Swedish Research Council (VR) funded Swedish partners in 2026. Its 2027 participation and any Ukrainian funder are unconfirmed. ERA4Health's 2026 calls (obesity, Trials4Health) are closed and off-topic.

### Inferences
- OHAMR runs on an annual cycle: pre-announcement around 20 Oct, opening mid-Nov, pre-proposals around 1–2 Feb, full proposals around mid-June, starts Dec–Apr. A JTC 2028 (theme unknown) would likely follow the same pattern (pre-announce Oct 2027, pre-proposals Feb 2028). Its theme could be closer to IPC/stewardship behaviour, and the consortium could lobby VR and the partnership for a behavioural/implementation topic.
- If Ukraine has no national funder in OHAMR, Ukrainian partners (ZDOROVI, Ternopil hospital, Bogomolets, NULES, Nursing Association) would have to join as self-funded or collaborating partners. That works against the equitable-partnership workstream.
- The JTC 2026 adherence topic was the closest OHAMR precedent for behavioural or implementation research. It is closed, and JTC 2027 drops that theme.

## 7. Next MFF (2028–2034) and FP10: timing implications for 2027–2028 calls

### Takeaway
2027 is the final call year of Horizon Europe, EU4Health and Erasmus+ 2021–2027. The Commission proposed the 2028–2034 MFF on 16 July 2025, including an FP10 of about €175bn and Erasmus+ at €40.8bn. Council negotiations are ongoing: the Irish Presidency's third negotiating box with figures came on 10 Oct 2026 and cut Horizon further. The target is a deal by the end of 2026. Expect a gap: the last Horizon/EU4Health/Erasmus+ deadlines fall in 2027, and the first FP10 and new-Erasmus+ calls are unlikely to have deadlines before mid-2028. A scaled-up follow-on should either target the 2027 calls or plan for FP10 2028 calls.
