# Non-Swedish national-government and bilateral funders (outside EU institutions) for "Empowering Ukrainian nurses to strengthen AMR response" (Uppsala/ReAct-led, start Jan 2027)

*How these notes were compiled and what limits them (read first):* Research date is 28 Oct 2026. This session could not open any funder website directly. Every direct page fetch failed with a DNS/allow-list error (nrfu.org.ua, nihr.ac.uk, klinikpartnerschaften.de, forskningsradet.no, estdev.ee, gov.uk), and the web-search budget ran out after about 40 queries. **So every deadline and amount below comes from search-engine summaries of the official pages (or of third-party aggregators where flagged), not from reading the pages myself.** Before anything goes into a proposal timeline, re-check each date on the URL given. "Fit" ratings are the analyst's own judgement against the project's four workstreams: evaluating the IPC/AMR course and nurse-led improvement projects (WS1), nursing curriculum assessment (WS2), a policy synthesis on nurses' roles, task-shifting and gender (WS3), and research capacity and doctoral pathways for Ukrainian nurses (WS4). "Lead" means the organisation that must apply. Sweden-based partners (Uppsala, KI) can almost never receive money from these schemes. The usual way in is to add a partner institution from the funder's country.

## 1. Ukraine's own funders: NRFU (domestic and bilateral joint calls), Ministry of Education and Science / Ministry of Health, Public Health Center of Ukraine

### Takeaway
As of 28 Oct 2026, no NRFU or NRFU-bilateral call is open that fits the project. All the 2026 NRFU calls closed between February and June 2026. The calls run on a regular annual cycle, though, and Ukrainian partners (Bogomolets NMU, NULES) could apply as lead in the spring 2027 round, which would fund only the Ukrainian side. The best-aligned multilateral instrument, the LUKE joint call (Ukraine plus national funders from 10 other countries, with "medical and health research" as a topic), does **not** include Sweden. Its next call is planned for autumn 2027. Sweden is also absent from every NRFU bilateral scheme found (Norway, Netherlands, Lithuania, Switzerland).

### Inferences
- **Predicted NRFU timing (from the 2025–2026 pattern):** President's grants open around January with deadlines in late February/early March 2027. The "Excellent Science", "Individual projects" and "Applied Science for Resilience and Recovery" calls look likely to open around March–April 2027 with deadlines around late April to May 2027, for projects starting in 2028. That is too late to co-fund year 1 of a January 2027 start, but could fund Ukrainian research costs in year 2 or a follow-on. This is extrapolated from two cycles only.
- NRFU bilateral calls seem to come about once a year per partner country (RCN: recruitment call 2025, then thematic mental-health call 2026; NWO: Hop-On rounds 2025 and 2026). A Norwegian partner such as NIPH, which already works with the PHC on AMR/IPC, would be the strongest route if RCN–NRFU announces another thematic health call. No such call has been announced.
- For LUKE call 2 (autumn 2027), the Swedish lead cannot be funded. The consortium would need, for example, a Finnish, Estonian, German or Polish partner plus a Latvian, Czech or Austrian one, with the Ukrainian partner funded by NRFU or NASU. That suits a follow-on (WS4 doctoral pathways, scaling the curriculum work) better than the core 2027 project.
- The Public Health Center, together with the Norwegian NIPH cooperation, matters more as a policy-uptake partner for WS3 than as a source of money.

## 2. United Kingdom: Fleming Fund / Fleming Initiative, NIHR Global Health Research, UKRI/MRC, British Council, GAMRIF

### Takeaway
UK government AMR and global-health money is essentially **not accessible** to a Ukraine nursing project in late 2026. The Fleming Fund ended in March 2026 after the 2025 cut in UK aid (ODA) from 0.5% to 0.3% of GNI. NIHR Global Health Research has refocused on sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia only. GAMRIF funds product and innovation pipelines. The only UK lines that were open to Ukraine in 2026 were small British Council higher-education partnership grants (Going Global Partnerships "Gender Equality Partnerships", £25k, Ukraine eligible, closed 21 Sep 2026) and UK–Ukraine networking schemes.

### Inferences
- In late 2026 the US is not a viable funder for a Swedish-led nursing AMR project. USAID no longer exists. CDC money goes through cooperative agreements with US-selected implementers or the MoH/PHC. Fogarty requires a US lead and faces elimination again in FY2027.
- The CDC/ICAP/WHO AMR-IPC footprint in Ternopil (labs, stewardship, IPC, colonisation screening) means the project should map and coordinate with any continuing CDC-funded activity at Ternopil Regional Clinical Hospital to avoid duplication. If that activity has ended, the project could present itself as filling a gap left by US withdrawal. Both scenarios are plausible; the 2026 status is unverified.

**Cross-cutting conclusions (all funders):**
- **Nothing open on 28 Oct 2026 fits well.** All relevant 2026 calls closed between January and September 2026. The ones still technically open (GIZ RPU community call, closing 30 Oct 2026; possibly the RCN–NRFU recruitment call) do not fit.
