## Q2. ICARS: funding/partnership model, implementation research calls, Ukraine as a partner country

### Takeaway
ICARS does **not run open competitive calls**. Projects begin with an Expression of Interest from a national **ministry**, partnered with a national research institute, followed by a concept note, an MoU with ICARS and a full proposal approved by ICARS Executive Management. ICARS already funds projects in the WHO European Region (Georgia, Kyrgyzstan) at roughly USD 0.5–0.73M each. **No Ukraine partnership or project was found.** Ukraine could become a partner if the Ministry of Health submits an EoI. Thematic fit (IPC, implementation research, NAP-aligned) is high.

### Cited Findings
- **Strategy 2026–2030.** Centred on country-led action, implementation research and sustainable solutions across One Health sectors. Focus areas include infection prevention and control, vaccination, diagnostics, antimicrobial stewardship and biosecurity. — [ICARS Strategy 2026–2030](https://icars-global.org/what-we-do/icars-strategy/)
- **Process.**
  1. The country submits an EoI with a cover letter from the responsible ministry (or ministries). That ministry is the main counterpart throughout.
  2. While drafting the EoI, the ministry engages one or more national research institutes.
  3. Concept note.
  4. MoU between ICARS and the ministry.
  5. Full project proposal, approved by ICARS Executive Management, which allocates the grant.
  6. The lead research institute implements, with ICARS advice and support.
  - — [ICARS – Apply for funding](https://icars-global.org/apply-for-funding/)
- **Eligibility.** An ICARS partner page states that countries on the OECD DAC list may apply for funding and technical partnerships, and that EoIs are accepted from ministries or groups of ministries working with national research institutions. — [ICARS partner page](https://icars-global.org/partner-test/)
- **Portfolio.** "Over 70 projects across more than 30 countries" (One Health Summit, April 2026). The 2021–2026 executive summary lists 75 projects in 33 countries/territories. — [ICARS at One Health Summit 2026](https://icars-global.org/icars-at-the-one-health-summit-2026/); [ICARS Executive Summary Q2 2026](https://icars-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ICARS-Executive-Summary_Q2-print.pdf)
- **European Region precedents:**
  - **Georgia.**
    - MoU signed 29 September 2020 with two ministries.
    - Projects (as reported in search extracts): gender equality and social inclusion, 2021–2024, USD 499,960; surgical antibiotic prophylaxis, 2022–2025, USD 524,464.
    - — [ICARS–Georgia](https://icars-global.org/icars-and-the-government-of-georgia-commit-fighting-resistance-antibiotics-together/)
  - **Kyrgyzstan.**
    - Mission Partner since 28 September 2022.
    - Children's respiratory-infection antibiotic-use project, "Region: Europe", January 2022 – March 2027, ICARS funding 725,534.15 (currency presumably USD); a second phase approved.
    - — [ICARS Kyrgyz Mission Partner](https://icars-global.org/kyrgyz-republic-mission-partner/); [Kyrgyzstan RTI project](https://icars-global.org/projects/kyrgyzstan-rti/)
- **Funding base.**
  - Denmark committed continued core funding to ICARS for 2027–2030.
  - The UK DHSC has funded a joint IDRC–ICARS AMR project in the Caribbean since 2025.
  - — [ICARS funding partners](https://icars-global.org/funding-partners/)
  - The Novo Nordisk Foundation funds an ICARS sustainable antibiotic-use roadmap for LMICs. — [ICARS–NNF](https://icars-global.org/icars-secures-funding-from-the-novo-nordisk-foundation-develop-a-sustainable-antibiotic-use-roadmap-in-low-and-middle-income-countries/)

### Inferences

## Q3. AMR Multi-Partner Trust Fund: funding rounds, Ukraine eligibility, NAP implementation support

### Takeaway
The AMR MPTF supports **country NAP implementation through Quadripartite agencies (WHO, FAO, WOAH, UNEP)**. Its second round of country projects was selected after independent external review, and **ten new projects launch in 2026**. Kyrgyzstan is the only European-region country in that round; **Ukraine is not included**. No open call exists. Ukraine shows up in an MPTF factsheet country list, but no Ukraine project was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Origins.** Established in 2019 by the Tripartite (FAO, WOAH, WHO) to help countries counter AMR through a One Health approach, collaborating across human, animal, food-systems, plant and environmental health. UNEP later joined to form the Quadripartite. — [UNDP MPTF Office – AMR MPTF](https://mptf.undp.org/fund/amr00); [MPTF launch news](https://mptf.undp.org/news/new-multi-partner-trust-fund-launched-combat-antimicrobial-resistance-globally-0)
- **Scale and donors.** The fund had supported 14 countries since 2019. In 2024 existing donors contributed, but no new donor commitments were made. — [AMR MPTF Annual Report 2024](https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2025-05/antimicrobial_resistance-2024_narrative_report.pdf)
- **Second round (projects launching 2026).**
  - An independent external expert review of second-round country proposals, prompted by feedback in the 2024 Mid-term Review, selected ten new projects for launch in 2026.
  - Approved concept notes: Egypt, Honduras, Kyrgyzstan, Lebanon, Nepal, Philippines, Sri Lanka, plus second phases in Ghana, Kenya and Zimbabwe.
  - — [AMR MPTF narrative report, 15 Jul 2025](https://www.qjsamr.org/docs/librariesprovider25/amr-mptf/15-07-2025-narrative-report---amr-mptf.pdf?sfvrsn=8750033_3)
- **The fund's own description.** It says 7 additional countries are "expected to receive multi-year funding from 2026", including Egypt, Ghana, Honduras, Kyrgyzstan, Lebanon, Nepal, Philippines and Sri Lanka, "pending expert, independent validation". Note that the count (7) and the list (8 names) do not match. — [QJS AMR – MPTF about](https://www.qjsamr.org/multi-partner-trust-fund/about)
- **Global Programme.** A new AMR MPTF Global Programme was approved in late 2025 (per search extract of the 2025 report). — [AMR MPTF narrative report 2025](https://www.qjsamr.org/docs/librariesprovider25/amr-mptf/15-07-2025-narrative-report---amr-mptf.pdf?sfvrsn=8750033_3)
- **Ukraine.** The MPTF Office factsheet includes Ukraine among country entries (alongside Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, Tajikistan, Türkiye, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan). No Ukraine project or funding figure was found. — [AMR MPTF factsheet](https://mptf.undp.org/factsheet/fund/AMR00)

### Inferences
- The factsheet's Ukraine entry probably reflects a multi-country or regional listing (for example, a Europe/Central Asia regional activity) or a generic country taxonomy, not a dedicated Ukraine project. Treat it as **unverified**.

### Inferences
- **UHF.** A UHF-eligible ZDOROVI could pursue a **separate humanitarian IPC service/training grant**: IPC supplies plus nurse training in frontline/evacuation-chain facilities under the Health Cluster. It could run alongside the research grant without paying for research costs. Achieving eligibility takes time and allocations are tied to HNRP priorities. This would be a **co-financing/sustainability lever**, not core funding.
- **Global Fund.** The TB-IPC priority opens a narrow niche: Principal Recipients (for example, the Alliance for Public Health or the Public Health Center) could integrate nurse IPC training in TB facilities from Jan 2027. That is low–medium fit.
- **Ternopil overlap with ICAP.** The partner Ternopil Regional Clinical Hospital sits in an ICAP/CDC AMR-IPC region. Coordinating with ICAP avoids duplication and strengthens the "builds on existing systems" argument. Note also that US funding to Ukraine has been volatile **[background, unverified]**.

## Q8. CEB, EBRD (non-commercial health) and NATO Science for Peace and Security

### Takeaway
**NATO SPS has the only open call in this inventory: the 2027-1 call closes 15 Nov 2026 (23:59 CET)**. Ukraine is an eligible partner country and the SPS programme's largest beneficiary since 2014. Projects need co-directors from a NATO member and a partner country, and the call is technology and security oriented (TRL 3–6). Fit for nurse AMR/IPC implementation research is weak unless reframed (for example, protection of personnel or medical CBRN/biological threats). CEB finances Ukrainian health infrastructure through MoH loans (EUR 100M, HEAL co-financing) and small trust-fund grants; there is no call. EBRD's AMR relevance in Ukraine is agricultural (an FAO–EBRD poultry antibiotic-use competition), relevant to NULES but not to nursing.

### Cited Findings
**NATO SPS**
- **2027-1 call (dated September 2026).**
  - Full proposals due **15 November 2026, 23:59 CET**, via the SPS grant platform.
  - Open to Multi-Year Projects (24–36 months, results aiming at TRL 3–6) and Events (Advanced Research Workshops, Advanced Training Courses, Advanced Study Institutes).
  - Applications aligned with any SPS Key Priority are welcome. Topics of particular interest include non-kinetic mitigation against unmanned systems; protection of critical infrastructure, supplies and personnel; maritime/undersea infrastructure resilience; and biotechnology (including convergence with AI).
  - **Ukraine is an eligible NATO partner country.** Each activity needs a NATO-country Project Director and a partner-country Project Director; all participants must be nationals of NATO members or eligible partners.
  - — [SPS Call 2027-1 PDF](https://www.nato.int/content/dam/nato/webready/documents/sps/grants/SPS-Call4proposals-2027-1.pdf); [SPS – How to apply](https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/organization/nato-structure/science-for-peace-and-security-hub/how-to-apply)
- **Earlier calls.**
  - 2026-1: deadline 31 Jan 2026; its text mentioned biosensors for disease diagnosis. — [SPS Call 2026-1 PDF](https://www.nato.int/content/dam/nato/webready/documents/sps/grants/SPS_call-for-proposals_2026-1.pdf); [NATO news, 23 May 2025](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/articles/news/2025/05/23/nato-science-for-peace-and-security-programme-launches-new-call-for-research-proposals)
  - Special call 2026-2 (counter-UAS): deadline 14 June 2026. — [SPS special call 2026-2 PDF](https://www.nato.int/content/dam/nato/webready/documents/sps/grants/sps-special-call-for-proposals-2026-2.pdf)

### Inferences
- **NATO SPS eligibility.** Sweden has been a NATO member since March 2024 **[background, unverified this session]**, so Uppsala or KI could provide the NATO-country Project Director, with a Ukrainian co-director (for example, Bogomolets).
- **NATO SPS fit.** SPS favours security-relevant science and technology at TRL 3–6. A nurse-education and implementation-research project fits poorly. The more credible SPS angles are:
  - an **Advanced Training Course** on IPC/AMR in mass-casualty and evacuation chains;
  - an **Advanced Research Workshop** on AMR as a security threat.
  - Either is smaller and quicker than a Multi-Year Project.
  - The 15 Nov 2026 deadline is only about 2.5 weeks away.
- **SPS cycle.** The general call deadline has been in winter (31 Jan 2026; 15 Nov 2026), with ad-hoc special calls. If the 15 Nov 2026 deadline is missed, expect a **2028-1 call around Sep–Nov 2027** (inference from two rounds).
- **CEB and EBRD.** CEB is government-to-government and infrastructure-focused, so not relevant beyond context (damaged infrastructure undermining IPC). EBRD's AMR activity in Ukraine is agri-food, a possible One Health side-link for NULES.
