Wednesday 7 Oct

Boat to Djurgården, Skansen, the Royal Palace, Gamla Stan and a view from Södermalm.

Together all day
  1. 09:30

    Breakfast at your place

    Before leaving, buy each of them an SL 72-hour ticket in the SL app. Started around 10:30, it lasts until Saturday morning and covers the metro, tram 7, the boat and the commuter train.

  2. 10:30

    Metro to Slussen

    Blue line to T-Centralen, then the red or green line two stops south to Slussen. About 25 minutes.

    Blue lineRedGreen
  3. 11:00

    SL boat to Djurgården

    Djurgårdsfärjan, line 82, from the quay at Slussen to Allmänna gränd. About 10 minutes on the water past Gamla Stan and Skeppsholmen. The SL ticket is valid. From the pier it is a 10-minute walk to Skansen's main entrance.

    Boat 82About every 15 min
  4. 11:20

    Skansen

    Open-air museum and Nordic zoo, with moose, bears, seals, the old town quarter and its craft workshops, and city views from the hilltop. Have lunch inside. Skansen closes early on weekdays in October, so it comes first.

    Open 10:00–15:00
  5. 14:00

    Boat back to Slussen

    Line 82 again from Allmänna gränd. It stops at Skeppsholmen on the way back, so it takes about 15 minutes.

    Boat 82
  6. 14:30

    Royal Palace

    10-minute walk from Slussen up Slottsbacken. The Royal Apartments and the Treasury take about an hour. It closes at 16:00, so don't linger at lunch.

    Open 10:00–16:00
  7. 16:00

    Fika at Skeppsbro Bageri

    On the Skeppsbron quay just below the palace. Cardamom bun and coffee by the water.

    Open until 18:00
  8. 16:45

    Gamla Stan walk

    Stortorget (point out the Stock Exchange building for Thursday), Mårten Trotzigs gränd, the narrowest alley at about 90 cm, then Västerlånggatan and Köpmangatan.

  9. 17:40

    Fjällgatan viewpoint

    Walk through Slussen and up to Fjällgatan on Södermalm, about 15 minutes. The view takes in Gamla Stan, Skeppsholmen and Djurgården in the last light. Sunset is about 18:05.

  10. 18:15

    Dinner at Hermans

    Vegetarian buffet a few doors along Fjällgatan, with the same view from the terrace and windows. Good odds for an aubergine dish. Listed as open until 20:00 Monday to Thursday, so arrive by about 18:30.

    Until ~20:00

If Wednesday gets long

  • Skip the palace rooms and walk the outer courtyard instead. The fika and the walk still fit.
  • If someone wants meat for dinner, keep Hermans for another trip and eat in Gamla Stan after the walk.

Thursday 8 Oct

Vasa Museum, the market hall, the Literature Prize announcement, City Hall, then sunset on Skinnarviksberget.

On their own until 16:30With you from 16:30

For your parents: every ride today is covered by the SL 72-hour ticket. Open the SL app or Google Maps for the next departure; the stops below are the ones to look for.

  1. 09:30

    Blue line to Kungsträdgården

    From Västra skogen, ride the blue line to the last stop, Kungsträdgården. About 15 minutes. The station is one of the metro's art stations.

    Blue line → Kungsträdgården
  2. 09:45

    Tram 7 to the Vasa

    Tram 7 towards Djurgården, get off at Nordiska museet/Vasa, about 10 minutes. On a dry morning, the walk along Strandvägen takes 25 minutes instead.

    Tram 7
  3. 10:00

    Vasa Museum

    The warship that sank on her maiden voyage in 1628 and was raised almost intact in 1961. Start with the short film, then walk the levels around the ship. Plan on about 1.5 hours.

    Open 10:00–17:00
  4. 11:35

    Tram 7 back to the centre

    From the same stop, towards Sergels torg (the last stop). From there, walk 5 minutes up to Hötorget.

    Tram 7 → Sergels torg
  5. 12:00

    Lunch at Hötorgshallen

    The indoor food hall on Hötorget. Go to the sandwich place, or browse the stalls. Outside is the fruit and flower market, and the blue Konserthuset, where the Nobel Prizes are presented every 10 December.

    Plan B: the supermarket lunch. Hemköp Stockholm City, below Åhléns City at Klarabergsgatan 50 (5 minutes' walk), has a hot food buffet and a salad bar.

    Opens 10:00Hemköp 07:00–23:00
  6. 12:40

    Green line to Gamla Stan

    From Hötorget station, two stops south to Gamla stan (any train towards Hagsätra, Farsta strand or Skarpnäck). Walk up to Stortorget, about 5 minutes.

    Green line → Gamla stan
  7. 13:00

    Nobel Prize in Literature, live

    The Swedish Academy announces this year's laureate at 13:00 or a little later. It happens in the old Stock Exchange on Stortorget, which also houses the Nobel Prize Museum, and the museum streams the announcement. Stay for the museum afterwards.

    Museum 11:00–17:00
  8. 14:30

    City Hall

    Walk 15–20 minutes over Riddarholmen. The courtyard and the waterfront garden are free. To see the Blue Hall and the Golden Hall, join the 15:00 English tour (45 min). Tickets are sold on the day at the City Hall shop: 150 kr, or 130 kr for seniors. The tower is closed this season.

    English tours hourly 10–15
  9. 15:55

    Red line to Södermalm

    Walk 10 minutes to T-Centralen and take the red line four stops south to Zinkensdamm (any train towards Norsborg or Fruängen).

    Red line → Zinkensdamm
  10. 16:30 · You join
  11. 16:30

    Fika at Svedjan

    Svedjan Bageri, Brännkyrkagatan 93, a few minutes from Zinkensdamm station.

    Open until 18:00
  12. 17:30

    Skinnarviksberget

    A short walk uphill to the highest natural point in the inner city. Sunset is about 18:00, off to the west over Riddarfjärden, with City Hall across the water. Afterwards you can walk Monteliusvägen if there is still light.

  13. 18:30

    Dinner at Tabbouli

    Lebanese meze at Tavastgatan 22, a few minutes down the hill. Smoky aubergine dishes like moutabal are a standard part of a Lebanese meze table, so this one is for your mum. Book a table: 08-429 82 55.

    Opens 17:00

Swaps for Thursday

  • Changing of the guard instead of Hötorgshallen. It starts at 12:15 in the palace's outer courtyard (Monday to Saturday, about 40 minutes). Take tram 7 back only as far as Kungsträdgården and walk 10 minutes to the palace. Stortorget is 3 minutes away for 13:00. Eat in Gamla Stan after the announcement.
  • Nationalmuseum instead of City Hall. On Thursdays it is open 11:00–20:00 and free from 17:00, so you could also go together after work in place of Skinnarviksberget.
  • Too tired? Drop City Hall and head to Södermalm early. Svedjan is a fine place to wait.

Friday 9 Oct

Karolinska with you before 10:00, a last fika in Vasastan, then straight to Arlanda.

With you until ~09:50Then on their own
  1. 08:00

    Breakfast and bags

    Pack before breakfast. The rest of the morning leads straight to the airport, so the bags come along.

  2. 09:00

    Karolinska Institutet with you

    A campus walk in Solna before you start work: Aula Medica, Biomedicum, and Nobel Forum, where this year's Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was announced on Monday.

  3. 09:50

    Over to Vasastan

    Lillebrors is about 2 km from campus. That's 25 minutes on foot through Hagastaden, or a short bus or taxi ride. Point them to whichever bus you'd take.

  4. 10:15

    Fika at Lillebrors Bageri

    Rörstrandsgatan 10, near S:t Eriksplan. Known for its cardamom buns. Closed only on Mondays.

    Open from 08:00 or earlier
  5. 10:50

    Walk to Odenplan

    About 10 minutes along Karlbergsvägen to Stockholm Odenplan station.

  6. 11:05

    Commuter train to Arlanda C

    Pendeltåg from Stockholm Odenplan towards Uppsala C, about 36 minutes to Arlanda C. On top of the SL ticket, each adult needs the Arlanda passage fee, about 150 kr, bought in the SL app before boarding. Check the exact departure the evening before.

    Pendeltåg → Arlanda C+ Arlanda fee
  7. 11:45

    At Arlanda

    Before the 12:00 target, with some margin.

If the bags are heavy or time is short

  • A taxi from Vasastan to Arlanda takes about 35–45 minutes. Agree on a fixed price before you get in.
  • Arlanda Express from Central Station takes 18 minutes and has a senior fare, but it means getting to Central first.

More ideas

For a gap in the day, a rainy hour, or a change of plan.

Food

  • Restaurant AubergineLinnégatan 38, Östermalm. A Mediterranean neighbourhood place, and the name speaks for itself. Check the current menu.
  • Östermalms SaluhallThe other big market hall, a restored 1888 brick building on Östermalmstorg. Good for a look and a snack. Hours not checked.
  • Supermarket lunchHemköp Stockholm City (Thursday plan B) is the easiest buffet in the centre.

Seeing

  • Solna Centrum metro stationOne stop from Västra skogen on the blue line, painted as a red sky over a green forest.
  • MonteliusvägenA cliff path near Skinnarviksberget that looks straight across to City Hall.
  • Changing of the guard12:15 Monday to Saturday in the palace's outer courtyard, about 40 minutes, free.

Tickets

  • SL 72-hour ticket360 kr for adults, and cheaper at the reduced price for anyone 65 or older. Covers everything in this plan except the Arlanda fee.
  • Arlanda passage feeAbout 150 kr per adult for the commuter train at Arlanda C. Buy it in the SL app.
