# Stack smaller grants around the Sida bid

As of 28 October 2026, no open call from a governmental, multilateral, NGO or philanthropic funder can pay for the whole SEK 13.9 million (about €1.2 million), 24-month project starting in January 2027. Only one instrument matches the concept note: Sida's 2026 call for **Health/SRHR development research cooperation in LMICs or Ukraine**, which offers SEK 8–14 million for 18–24 months, implemented January 2027 to December 2028. Its concept-note stage closed on **16 August 2026** ([Sida](https://sida.se/en/for-partners/calls-and-announcements); [SweDev](https://swedev.dev/sida-call-for-concept-notes-health-and-srhr-research-partnerships-in-lmics-and-ukraine/)). If the team submitted, the full-proposal stage matters most. If it did not, Sweden has no equivalent window. A 2027 repeat is uncertain: Sida's research budget has fallen to SEK 246 million, the Ukraine strategy expires in December 2027, and the September 2026 election has not yet produced a government. Six calls are technically open, but only four are relevant: MSCA Doctoral Networks (24 November), NATO Science for Peace and Security (15 November), a small ESCMID grant (18 November, unverified) and a VR visiting-researcher grant (10 November, which probably excludes Ukraine). None can carry the core project. The next six months bring the final calls of the EU's 2021–2027 programmes:

- the One Health AMR partnership's 2027 call (opens 18 November, pre-proposals 1 February 2027);
- Erasmus+ capacity building (likely February 2027);
- MSCA Staff Exchanges (15 April 2027);
- the EU4Health work programme adopted on 19 October.

Two Swedish windows also open: the Swedish Institute's Ukraine programme (likely December 2026 to spring 2027) and Riksbankens Jubileumsfond (January 2027). None of these can start before mid or late 2027, so they suit components and follow-on work. The most valuable partnership step is to formalise links with five bodies: Folkhälsomyndigheten's AMR Contain programme, which Sweden already funds as aid, Socialstyrelsen, the Ukrainian Ministry of Health's Centre for Nursing Development, the Public Health Center of Ukraine, and Ternopil National Medical University.

## Sida's 2026 call is the only instrument built for this budget

The concept note's figures sit just inside the call's ceilings: SEK 13.9 million against a SEK 14 million maximum, and 24 months, the longest duration allowed, starting in January 2027. Sida's call opened on 25 May 2026 and took concept notes until **16 August 2026, 17:00 CEST** ([Sida calls and announcements](https://sida.se/en/for-partners/calls-and-announcements)). It describes itself as the first step in identifying "two or more partnerships", each receiving **SEK 8–14 million for 18–24 months** between January 2027 and December 2028 ([SweDev](https://swedev.dev/sida-call-for-concept-notes-health-and-srhr-research-partnerships-in-lmics-and-ukraine/); [fundsforNGOs](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/research-2/applications-open-for-health-and-srhr-research-concept-sweden/)). A partnership must combine Swedish organisations with institutions from LMICs or Ukraine, and at least one member must be a legal entity able to sign with Sida, which Uppsala University satisfies as lead. Four things are not public: the full-proposal deadline, how many concept notes go forward, the decision date, and the rules on indirect costs and on transfers to Ukrainian partners. If the concept note went in by 16 August, the most useful single action is to ask the Sida programme officer for that timetable and those rules. If it did not, there is no open Sida window and no other instrument of comparable size.

Research funding around the call is shrinking even as aid to Ukraine grows. Sida's research budget fell from just over SEK 900 million to about SEK 440 million in 2023 and now stands at **SEK 246 million** ([Tidningen Curie](https://www.tidningencurie.se/nyheter/forskare-varnar-nedskarningarna-inom-global-halsa-kommer-att-kosta-liv)). Concord, the Swedish NGO confederation for development, calculates a **73% fall in development research funding over five years** ([Concord](https://concord.se/analys-regleringsbrev-till-sida-for-2026)). In June 2023 the government stopped new development research decisions through the Swedish Research Council (VR), which ended Swedish Research Links ([VR](https://www.vr.se/english/applying-for-funding/calls/2022-11-10-network-grant---swedish-research-links.html)).

Aid to Ukraine, by contrast, is guaranteed. The 2026 budget bill sets civil aid to Ukraine at **at least SEK 10 billion a year in 2026–2027** and SEK 10.5 billion in 2028 ([Budget bill 2026](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/proposition/budgetpropositionen-for-2026-utgiftsomrade-7_hd031d9/html/)). However, the reconstruction and reform strategy that governs this aid expires on **31 December 2027** ([Ukraine strategy 2023–2027](https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/84f9c0c646d44de4b5e0d2ad67893be1/strategi-for-sveriges-uppbyggnads--och-reformsamarbete-med-ukraina-2023-2027.pdf)).

The political picture is unsettled. The 13 September election gave the opposition bloc 176 seats against 173 for the Tidö parties, and Prime Minister Kristersson resigned on 17 September. No new government had taken office in the sources found ([Valmyndigheten](https://www.val.se/english/election-results/elections-to-the-riksdag-and-regional-and-municipal-councils/election-results-2026); [Wikipedia](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riksdagsvalet_i_Sverige_2026)). A repeat research call in 2027 could therefore go either way: a new government might restore development research, or a transitional budget might freeze it. No policy announcement exists either way.

For the full proposal, the practical lesson is to lean on the Ukraine strategy's reform and EU-accession logic, because that is where Swedish money is growing. That means presenting AMR and infection prevention and control (IPC) as areas where Ukraine must align with EU law on its path to membership, and nursing-workforce reform as part of health-system reform.

Sweden's other Ukraine instruments are about ten times smaller and mostly pay the Swedish side. The **Swedish Institute Ukraine Cooperation Programme (SIUCP)** funds cooperation projects of **SEK 1–2 million over 12–24 months** and preparation grants of SEK 100,000–200,000. The main applicant must be Swedish, with at least one Ukrainian legal entity as formal partner. The programme has 111 ongoing projects and an annual budget of SEK 60–70 million ([SI programme page](https://si.se/en/apply/funding-grants/si-ukraine-cooperation-programme-2/)). Its deadline has moved earlier each year: mid-May in 2024, 7 May in 2025 and 2 March in 2026, when the call opened on 5 December 2025 ([SI 2026 call](https://si.se/en/new-call-for-applications-within-the-si-ukraine-cooperation-programme-now-open/)). A 2027 call opening within the next one to two months is therefore plausible but not announced. The 2026 themes, gender equality and EU accession, would suit the gender and doctoral-pathway components.

The closest Swedish research instrument is VR's **grant for recruiting visiting researchers from Ukraine within the infection area**: SEK 2 million for 12 months, with rolling applications from 1 April to 29 September 2026. The money goes to the Swedish host, and whether the grant recurs depends on VR's 2027 instructions from the government ([VR call](https://www.vr.se/english/applying-for-funding/calls/2026-03-09-grant-for-recruiting-visiting-researchers-from-ukraine-within-the-infection-area.html)). The SI Baltic Sea and Eastern Partnership Programme offers training grants of up to SEK 3 million, but Ukraine may join only multilateral projects, so a Sweden–Ukraine project would need a third partner, for example from Moldova or Georgia ([SI 2026 instructions](https://si.se/app/uploads/2026/04/siepro_instructions_en_260417-v2.pdf)).

If Sida declines, these grants combined could fund doctoral mobility, a network and a gender strand. They could not fund the research at the project's core: the course evaluation, the curriculum assessment and the policy synthesis.

## Four calls are open today, and none can carry the core

The open calls that matter are few, close soon and touch only the edges of the project.

- **MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026.** The call closes on **24 November 2026** at 17:00 Brussels time, with €593.03 million for about 130 networks ([REA](https://rea.ec.europa.eu/funding-and-grants/horizon-europe-marie-sklodowska-curie-actions/msca-doctoral-networks_en)). Results come in April 2027 and projects start in August–September 2027 ([RAISE DN slides](https://digital-ai-finance.github.io/raise-doctoral-network-for-AI-driven-scientific-discovery/raise-dn-2026-slides.pdf)). A network of nursing and IPC doctoral candidates across Sweden, Ukraine and another EU country fits the doctoral-pathways component. Four weeks is too little to build a new consortium, though. The realistic options are to join an existing network as a partner or to prepare for the round expected in November 2027.
- **NATO Science for Peace and Security (SPS) call 2027-1.** It closes on **15 November 2026, 23:59 CET**. Ukraine is an eligible partner country, and each project needs co-directors from a NATO member and a partner country. The call favours security-relevant technology at technology readiness levels 3–6 ([SPS Call 2027-1](https://www.nato.int/content/dam/nato/webready/documents/sps/grants/SPS-Call4proposals-2027-1.pdf)). Only its event formats are credible here: an Advanced Training Course on IPC in mass-casualty and evacuation chains, or an Advanced Research Workshop on AMR as a security threat. Uppsala or KI could supply the NATO-member co-director. The grant ceiling was not found.
- **ESCMID LMIC grant.** A grant from ESCMID, the European infectious-diseases society, with an 18 November deadline appears only on a third-party listing and may be worth about €1,300 ([Atom Grants](https://atomgrants.com/grant/escmid-lmic-grant)). It could send a Ukrainian IPC nurse to training, provided Ukraine counts as a low- or middle-income country under ESCMID rules.
- **VR general visiting-researcher grant.** It is open until 10 November but targets researchers based outside Europe, which probably excludes Ukraine ([VR press release](https://via.tt.se/pressmeddelande/4181441/vi-utlyser-bidrag-for-rekrytering-av-forskare-utanfor-europa-aven-2026?publisherId=3237260&lang=sv)).

Several other opportunities are open in name only. The Pandemic Fund's fourth call accepts proposals until 31 March 2027, but only from 15 named high-risk countries, and Ukraine is not among them ([Pandemic Fund](https://www.thepandemicfund.org/news/announcement/pandemic-fund-launches-fourth-call-proposals-targeting-high-risk-high-need-countries)). The COST Open Call collection closed at 12:00 CET today ([COST](https://www.cost.eu/uploads/2026/03/2026-1-COST_Open_Call_Announcement.pdf)). A GIZ rehabilitation call closing on 30 October is open only to municipalities. The Research Council of Norway (RCN) and Ukraine's National Research Foundation (NRFU) run a recruitment scheme, but it only tops up ongoing Norwegian projects ([RCN](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/call-for-proposals/2025/recruit-researcher-ukraine-researcher-project/)).

Two major doors have closed or close tomorrow. From **29 October 2026**, Wellcome accepts lead applicants for its Discovery Research schemes only from the UK and from LMICs in Africa, South Asia and South-East Asia. That excludes both Sweden and Ukraine ([Wellcome](https://wellcome.org/research-funding/guidance/prepare-to-apply/eligibility-information-grant-applicants)). Elrha was the natural funder for humanitarian health research, but it closed both R2HC (Research for Health in Humanitarian Crises) and the Humanitarian Innovation Fund in March 2026 and lists no open calls ([Elrha](https://www.elrha.org/funding-opportunities); [Elrha R2HC closure](https://www.elrha.org/news-blogs/closing-the-r2hc-chapter-reflections-on-a-transformational-programme)).

UK government AMR money has also gone. The Fleming Fund ended in March 2026, and the global health research programme of the UK's National Institute for Health and Care Research (NIHR) now targets sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia ([Lancet Microbe](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanmic/article/PIIS2666-5247(25)00167-3/fulltext); [NIHR](https://www.nihr.ac.uk/news/nihr-announces-global-health-research-calls-2026)).

## The EU's last 2021–2027 calls land between November and April

The next six months are crowded because 2027 is the final call year of Horizon Europe, EU4Health and Erasmus+ in their 2021–2027 form. The Commission proposed the 2028–2034 EU budget in July 2025. The Irish Presidency tabled its third draft of the negotiating text, the first with figures, on 10 October 2026, and the aim is a deal by the end of 2026 ([Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/the-eu-s-long-term-budget-2028-2034/timeline-eu-long-term-budget-2028-2034/)). The proposed €175 billion for FP10, the next research framework programme, faces further proposed cuts ([EUA](https://www.eua.eu/news/eua-news/mff-horizon-europe-hit-by-additional-proposed-cuts-eu-member-states-must-protect-investment-in-r-i-and-education.html)). The first deadlines under FP10 and the next Erasmus+ programme are unlikely before mid-2028.

Just as important, no EU route can fund a January 2027 start. Likely start dates range from August 2027 (MSCA Doctoral Networks) to late 2027 or early 2028 (Erasmus+ capacity building, the One Health AMR partnership, MSCA Staff Exchanges). EU money should therefore pay for components and a follow-on project, not the core.

### One Health AMR partnership: 2027 joint call

The **European Partnership on One Health AMR (OHAMR)** pre-announced its 2027 joint transnational call, **"Understand, Model and Stop AMR Transmission"**, on 19 October 2026. It has about **€28 million** from 33 funders in 26 countries. The call opens on **18 November 2026**, an applicant webinar follows on 25 November, and pre-proposals are due on **1 February 2027** ([OHAMR](https://ohamr.eu/2026/10/19/pre-announcment-call-to-advance-research-on-amr-transmission/); [HRB](https://www.hrb.ie/funding-scheme/eup-ohamr-2027/)). The three topics touch IPC only indirectly, so the fit is medium to low:

- modelling how AMR spreads;
- the body's own bacteria and selective decolonisation;
- reducing resistant organisms in environmental hotspots.

Each consortium needs at least three partners requesting funding, from three eligible countries, with at least two in EU Member States or Associated Countries. Each partner is paid by its own national funder under national rules ([NIHR](https://www.nihr.ac.uk/funding/horizon-europe-european-partnership-one-health-antimicrobial-resistance-joint-transnational-call-2026/97504)).

Two unknowns decide whether to apply:

- **Swedish funding.** VR funded Swedish partners in 2026; a search summary suggested up to SEK 5 million each, which needs checking ([VR closed call](https://www.vr.se/5.68489fa3199c8f0e1c31f30e.html)). Whether VR joins the 2027 call is not confirmed.
- **Ukrainian funding.** No Ukrainian funder was named in 2026. Without one, the Ukrainian partners would go unfunded.

OHAMR has run a steady yearly cycle: pre-announcement in October, pre-proposals in February. That makes a 2028 call the most predictable EU channel across the budget transition. The team could lobby for a theme on IPC practice and behaviour.

### Erasmus+ capacity building and MSCA Staff Exchanges

The two best-fitting EU instruments open next.

**Erasmus+ Capacity Building in Higher Education (CBHE)** fits the curriculum component best. Ukraine belongs to the Neighbourhood East region, and the 2026 round selected **22 projects involving 105 Ukrainian organisations** ([EEAS Delegation to Ukraine](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/ukraine/22-new-projects-build-capacities-105-organisations-ukraine-under-eus-erasmus-programme_en)). The National Erasmus+ Office in Ukraine says the 2027 calls open in November 2026 ([Erasmus+ Ukraine](https://erasmusplus.org.ua/en/opportunities/erasmus-during-the-war-in-ukraine/)). The 2026 deadline was 10 February, so the last deadline under the current programme is likely in early to mid February 2027. In 2026, projects in the structural-reform strand (Strand 3) could request €600,000–€1 million over 36–48 months ([Lovguiden summary of EACEA](https://www.lovguiden.dk/en/det-offentlige/eacea-uddannelse-audiovisuel-og-kultur/2025-12-18-erasmus-cbhe-2026-information-om-tilskud-til-videregaaende-uddannelse)).

**MSCA Staff Exchanges 2027** opens on **15 December 2026** and closes on **15 April 2027**, with a provisional budget of about €95 million ([Research Ireland](https://www.researchireland.ie/marie-sklodowska-curie-actions/funding); [KI staff page](https://staff.ki.se/staff-exchange)). It funds secondments of both academic and non-academic staff, which suits exchanges among Uppsala, KI, Bogomolets, NULES, the Ternopil hospital, ZDOROVI and the Nursing Association. It does not pay core research salaries.

### EU4Health

**EU4Health** became relevant on 19 October 2026, when the Commission adopted the 2026 work programme (decision C(2026) 7403). It is worth more than €680 million, including **€111.03 million for health systems and the healthcare workforce** ([European Commission](https://health.ec.europa.eu/publications/2026-eu4health-work-programme_en); [EPF](https://www.eu-patient.eu/news/latest-epf-news/2026/adoption-of-the-2026-eu4health-work-programme/)). Ukraine has been associated with the programme since July 2022, so Ukrainian universities, hospitals and NGOs can apply on the same terms as Member State applicants ([European Commission](https://health.ec.europa.eu/funding/eu4health-programme-2021-2027-vision-healthier-european-union/association-agreement-ukraine_en)). No calls had appeared by 28 October. Going by the 2025 pattern, they should be published between November 2026 and the first quarter of 2027.

These are action grants, not research grants. They would suit scaling up the nurse IPC course if a topic on the workforce or IPC training appears. Operating grants for EU health NGOs total only €1.8 million ([EPHA](https://epha.org/while-eu4health-operating-grants-return-funding-and-decision-making-must-improve/)). EU-JAMRAI 2, the joint action on AMR and healthcare-associated infections, includes Ukraine. It is not open to applications, but findings can feed into it through Ukraine's national health authority ([RIVM](https://www.rivm.nl/en/international-projects/eu-jamrai2)).

### Horizon Europe health cluster, Widening and COST

Horizon Europe's health cluster fits poorly. The 2027 single-stage health calls reportedly open on 29 October 2026 with a **17 February 2027** deadline, though older text gave 13 April 2027 ([Accelopment](https://accelopment.com/blog/horizon-europe-health-2027-calls/)). The only AMR topic funds new antimicrobials, at about €8–10 million per project ([Innovate UK Business Connect](https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/opportunities/horizon-europe-2026-2027-health-cluster-1/)).

The Widening strand, which builds research capacity in less research-intensive countries, fits better in purpose. Ukraine is a Widening country, and the Institute of Molecular Biology and Genetics in Kyiv won **SURE-AMR**, a Twinning project worth €499,173, to build One Health AMR research capacity ([CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101160053)). But the 2026 Twinning call closed on 9 April ([Horizont Evropa CZ](https://www.horizontevropa.cz/en/newsletter/widening-participation-strengthening-era/widening-participation-spreading-excellence/yiifnews/3922)), and no 2027 Twinning round was confirmed. The next confirmed Widening deadline is ERA Chairs, on 14 September 2027.

COST, which Ukraine joined as a full member in 2022, funds networking only. Its next collection date is likely in late October 2027 ([COST](https://www.cost.eu/members-georgia-ukraine/)).

## Outside the EU, money follows national partners and relationships

Beyond the EU, almost every government scheme requires an applicant from the funder's own country. The real question is therefore "which partner?" rather than "which call?".

**Norway is the strongest strategic match.** Three facts make it so:

- its Nansen health agreement with Ukraine (NOK 53 million, December 2024 to December 2027) prioritises AMR;
- Norway helped build the AMR unit at the Public Health Center of Ukraine;
- the Norwegian Institute of Public Health (NIPH) runs an institutional cooperation on AMR and IPC with Ukraine's Ministry of Health, the Public Health Center and the National Health Service of Ukraine until 2027 ([Government of Norway](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/whats-new/norway-facilitates-rehabilitation-of-injured-children-and-mental-health-care-efforts-in-ukraine/id3096160/); [NIPH](https://www.fhi.no/en/cristin-projects/ongoing/public-health-collaboration-between-ukraine-and-norway/)).

Norway's competitive money flows through two channels, and neither has announced a new round ([HK-dir](https://hkdir.no/en/programmes-and-grant-schemes/nansen-edu-norwegian-ukrainian-cooperation-in-higher-education); [RCN](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/call-for-proposals/2026/researcher-project-mental-health-ukraine-wartime/)):

- **HK-dir's Nansen EDU** programme: up to NOK 5 million per project, led by an accredited Norwegian university with at least two Ukrainian universities. The last deadline was 2 March 2026.
- **Joint calls by the Research Council of Norway and NRFU**: NOK 2–6 million, with exactly one Norwegian and one Ukrainian partner.

**Germany offers the best route for hospital practice.** The hospital-partnership programme Klinikpartnerschaften, funded by the German development ministry (BMZ) and run by GIZ, includes Ukrainian hospitals and a special funding window for Ukraine. A German hospital, university or NGO must sign the grant ([klinikpartnerschaften.de](https://klinikpartnerschaften.de/in-ukraine); [BMZ](https://www.bmz.de/de/themen/klinikpartnerschaften)). Its 2027 call dates could not be verified.

**Estonia** is a smaller but real option. ESTDEV, Estonia's development cooperation centre, funded a record 13 Ukraine projects worth €2.3 million in 2026, with "modernising the health care sector" as a priority ([ESTDEV](https://estdev.ee/en/articles/winners-2026-ukraine-call-proposals-announced-record-number-projects-receive-funding)). Tallinn Health Care College already runs health-education work for Ukraine with Belgium's development agency Enabel ([ESTDEV](https://estdev.ee/en/articles/estonia-belgium-and-ukraine-sign-cooperation-agreement-advance-health-care-education)).

**Ukraine's own funders** can pay the Ukrainian side. The National Research Foundation of Ukraine (NRFU) closed all its 2026 calls between February and June. Its spring cycle should let Bogomolets or NULES lead a Ukrainian-funded research component starting in 2028 ([NRFU](https://nrfu.org.ua/en/contests-posts-en/2026-08-call-applied-science-for-the-resilience-and-recovery-of-ukraine/)). The LUKE joint call covers medical and health research but excludes Sweden; its second call opens in autumn 2027 ([NRFU LUKE](https://nrfu.org.ua/en/news-en/luke-joint-call/)).

**The United States is not a viable funder.** USAID was dissolved in July 2025 ([AP via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/usaid-officially-ends-operation-remaining-093240430.html)), and the 2027 federal budget request again proposes eliminating NIH's Fogarty International Center ([KFF](https://www.kff.org/global-health-policy/global-health-funding-in-the-fy-2027-presidents-budget-request/)).

**Multilateral bodies are partners more than grant-makers.**

- **ICARS** (Copenhagen) runs no open calls. A national ministry submits an expression of interest together with a national research institute; a concept note, a memorandum of understanding and a full proposal follow ([ICARS](https://icars-global.org/apply-for-funding/)). Its European-region projects in Georgia and Kyrgyzstan received about **USD 0.5–0.73 million** each ([ICARS–Georgia](https://icars-global.org/icars-and-the-government-of-georgia-commit-fighting-resistance-antibiotics-together/); [ICARS Kyrgyzstan](https://icars-global.org/projects/kyrgyzstan-rti/)). Its 2026–2030 strategy prioritises IPC and implementation research ([ICARS strategy](https://icars-global.org/what-we-do/icars-strategy/)), but no ICARS work in Ukraine was found. That scale suits a scale-up phase led by Ukraine's Ministry of Health, with Bogomolets or Ternopil National Medical University as the research institute.
- **WHO Ukraine** sought US$42 million in 2026, leads a Health Cluster of more than 200 partners, and has trained 142 professionals in national IPC schools ([WHO/Europe](https://www.who.int/europe/news/item/06-02-2026-ukraine--who-seeks-us-42-million-in-2026-to-protect-health-care-as-war-enters-its-fifth-year); [WHO](https://www.who.int/europe/news/item/16-04-2025-strengthening-knowledge-and-skill-in-infection-prevention-and-control-in-ukraine)). It is the natural technical partner, but it has no public call for implementing partners.
- **TDR and WHO/Europe implementation-research grants** are small: US$10,000–15,000. Ukraine was eligible in the last round, which closed on 31 October 2024 ([Astana Medical University](https://amu.edu.kz/en/infocenter/news/13796/?changeLang=true); [TDR](https://tdr.who.int/newsroom/news/item/05-05-2025-impact-grants-awarded-for-implementation-research-in-the-european-region)).
- **The AMR Multi-Partner Trust Fund**'s second round, launching in 2026, does not include Ukraine ([AMR MPTF](https://www.qjsamr.org/docs/librariesprovider25/amr-mptf/15-07-2025-narrative-report---amr-mptf.pdf?sfvrsn=8750033_3)).
- **The OCHA Ukraine Humanitarian Fund** could give ZDOROVI a separate grant for humanitarian IPC services and training, once ZDOROVI passes the fund's five-step eligibility process ([UHF](https://www.unocha.org/about-ukraine-humanitarian-fund/eligibility)).
- **The Global Fund**'s guidance on tuberculosis for its 2026–2028 funding cycle lists IPC as a priority area, reachable only through the organisations that implement its grants in Ukraine ([Global Fund](https://resources.theglobalfund.org/media/1b3b0pem/cr_gc8-tb-prioritization_guidance_en.pdf)).

**Philanthropy offers relationships more than open calls.**

- **The Burdett Trust for Nursing** is the highest-value target. It has funded palliative-care nursing across Ukraine, adopted a new five-year strategy in February 2026, and is developing a "Nursing Futures" programme whose grants are "not intended for standalone initiatives" ([Burdett Trust](https://www.btfn.org.uk/); [Burdett strategy](https://www.btfn.org.uk/2026/02/18/grant-making-strategy/)). The pitch should frame the project as delivering a Burdett priority. Whether non-UK organisations can apply is unconfirmed.
- **The Novo Nordisk Foundation (NNF)** runs a call, "Informing humanitarian response through research, learning and innovation", that names Ukraine as a target geography and pays **DKK 1–5 million for up to two years**. The main applicant must be an international humanitarian organisation, with universities as co-applicants ([NNF](https://novonordiskfonden.dk/en/grant/informing-humanitarian-response-through-research-learning-and-innovation/)). The 2025 round ran from 17 January to 16 April, and no 2026 round was found ([fundsforNGOs](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/latest-funds-for-ngos/cfps-informing-humanitarian-response-through-research-learning-and-innovation/)). NNF's AMR calls target diagnostics and tools and fit poorly ([NNF Catalyst](https://novonordiskfonden.dk/en/grant/infectious-diseases-catalyst-grants/)).
- **Riksbankens Jubileumsfond** (RJ) has confirmed an RJ Projects round for 2027. The 2026 window ran from 7 to 27 January. RJ funds social sciences and humanities for up to three years, which suits a strand on nursing leadership, gender and how evidence reaches policy ([RJ](https://www.rj.se/en/funding/rj-projects-2026/)).
- **The International Renaissance Foundation** in Kyiv accepts proposals in Ukrainian under its Public Health Programme. It can also fund "socially significant" projects outside its competitions ([IRF](https://www.irf.ua/en/program/health/)).
- **Smaller grants** suit individual add-ons. The Swedish Society of Nursing's project grants closed on 15 October 2026. ESCMID research grants pay up to €20,000, but the 2027 cycle is limited to fungal, parasitic and viral topics ([SSF](https://swenurse.se/fonder-och-stipendier/projektmedel/svensk-sjukskoterskeforenings-projektmedel); [ESCMID](https://www.escmid.org/science-research/grants-awards/research-grants/individual-research-grant/)).

Two names drop out. Vårdalstiftelsen, once Sweden's foundation for care research, was wound up in 2015 ([Vårdalstiftelsen](https://www.vardal.se/2015/03/31/vardalstiftelsen-laggs-ner/)). Commercial AMR investment vehicles such as the AMR Action Fund are excluded by the brief.

## Coherence with Swedish aid to Ukraine is the strongest partnership lever

The most important partnership finding is that Sweden already funds an AMR and IPC programme in Ukrainian hospitals: Folkhälsomyndigheten's **AMR Contain (2025–2027)**. It is paid for from Swedish development aid and runs under Socialstyrelsen's mandate to coordinate Swedish health support to Ukraine ([Folkhälsomyndigheten/Strama slides](https://strama.se/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/stodprogram-till-sjukhus-i-ukraina-inom-vardhygien-och-arbetet-mot-antibiotikaresistens-amr-contain-stephan-260512.pdf); [Strama](https://strama.se/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/amr-contain-ukraina.pdf)). In outline:

- **Partners:** the Public Health Center of Ukraine, Kharkiv National Medical University, the Kharkiv and Lviv regional centres for disease control, and regional hospitals.
- **Activities:** IPC training, treatment guidelines, laboratory capacity and AMR data. It is also piloting an app for hygiene guidelines and training.
- **Coordination:** with Denmark's Statens Serum Institut and Norway's NIPH.
- **End date:** the programme ends in **December 2027** and has said it wants to plan for what continues afterwards.

Socialstyrelsen's Sida-funded Ukraine programme totals **SEK 129.5 million for 2025–2027** ([Government of Sweden](https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/02/utokat-stod-till-sociala-och-medicinska-reformer-i-ukraina/)). ReAct's own project supporting Ukraine's national AMR action plan already runs "in collaboration with the Swedish public health agency" ([ReAct](https://www.reactgroup.org/news-and-views/news-and-opinions/year-2025/react-project-building-resilience-against-amr-amid-conflict-in-ukraine/)), so formalising the link costs little. The project can present itself as AMR Contain's nursing, education and research complement. Because it runs from January 2027 to December 2028, a year past AMR Contain's end, that framing answers Sida's questions on coherence and sustainability at once.

Ukrainian ownership rests on three state bodies and one university.

- **The Centre for Nursing Development of the Ministry of Health** owns the Strategic Directions for Nursing Development (November 2024), the 2025–2027 Roadmap and the new professional standards for nursing. Its director, Kateryna Komar, also appears as Chief Nursing Officer in 2025 coverage ([TNMU](https://www.tdmu.edu.ua/en/?p=178104); [Ukrainian Red Cross](https://redcross.org.ua/en/news/2025/04/110672/)). A 2026 ReAct interview, however, names Alla Kuzhel as Ukraine's chief nurse ([ReAct](https://www.reactgroup.org/news-and-views/news-and-opinions/year-2026/ukrainian-chief-nurse-alla-kuzhel-antibiotic-resistance-is-not-a-problem-of-the-future/)). Confirm who holds which title before addressing letters.
- **The Public Health Center of Ukraine** implements the State Strategy for Combating AMR to 2030. The strategy's operational plan for 2024–2026 expires this year, which gives the policy synthesis a chance to inform the next plan. Separately, Ministry of Health Order No. 1328 of September 2026 has just set new standards for rational antimicrobial use ([State Expert Centre](https://www.dec.gov.ua/en/news/v-ukrayini-onovleno-pidhody-do-raczionalnogo-zastosuvannya-protymikrobnyh-likarskyh-zasobiv-u-zakladah-ohorony-zdorovya/)).
- **The National Health Service of Ukraine** oversees the IPC plans that every health facility must submit ([WHO/Europe](https://www.who.int/europe/news/item/23-08-2023-who-infection-prevention-and-control-minimum-requirements-adopted-at-national-level-in-ukraine)).
- **Ternopil National Medical University** runs a PhD programme in nursing (specialty 223) and publishes the journal *Nursing*. It is in the same city as the hospital partner and is the natural Ukrainian lead for future Twinning or Erasmus+ capacity-building bids ([TNMU](https://www.tdmu.edu.ua/en/?p=166368); [TNMU *Nursing*](https://ojs.tdmu.edu.ua/nursing/article/view/15734)).

Technical partners matter as much for avoiding duplication as for credibility.

- **WHO.** On 11 May 2026, WHO published the **AMR and IPC curriculum assessment tool for nursing and midwifery education**, the tool the curriculum component will apply ([WHO](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240122369)). That gives WHO headquarters and WHO/Europe's nursing unit reasons to join an advisory board. Sida's own 2026 Junior Professional Officer post on midwifery education with WHO in Ukraine is a Sida–WHO link the application can cite ([Sida JPO ToR](https://cdn.sida.se/app/uploads/2026/06/03133448/JPO-2026_Terms-of-Reference_EU-Midwifery-Ukraine.pdf)).
- **ICAP in Ternopil.** ICAP at Columbia University has run IPC quality improvement, funded by the US CDC, at the Ternopil regional centre for disease control. In February–March 2026 it began screening for carbapenem-resistant organisms in four hospitals ([ICAP](https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icap-supports-quality-improvement-for-infection-prevention-and-control-in-ukrainian-health-care-facilities); [ICAP](https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icap-supports-ukraines-wartime-fight-against-antimicrobial-resistance/)). The nurse-led improvement projects should build on that baseline without depending on US funding, whose continuity into 2027 is unknown.
- **Scientific advisers.** Two research groups have documented the urgency. Lund University and EUCAST, working with colleagues in Vinnytsia, found that about 6% of samples from 141 war victims resisted every antibiotic tested ([Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/article/alarming-antibiotic-resistance-discovered-war-torn-ukraine)). Kharkiv National Medical University ran a multi-hospital study of multidrug-resistant infections ([Annals of Mechnikov Institute](https://journals.uran.ua/ami/article/view/356788)). Both groups would make strong scientific advisers.

## If the budget and timeline can change

Most funders above were ruled out by the concept note's fixed shape: SEK 13.9 million, 24 months, starting January 2027. The team has since said that budget and timeline can be adjusted. That changes the picture in three ways. Figures carry the same "verify before use" status. The facts added in this section come from follow-up searches on 28 October 2026 and are cited inline.

**A later start opens a second hub, but only if the team applies in the next four months.** The strongest alternative to Sida is **Erasmus+ Capacity Building in Higher Education (CBHE)**.

- **Timing:** the last call under the current programme is expected in mid-November 2026, with a deadline expected in early February 2027 ([TU Dresden notice](https://tu-dresden.de/forschung-transfer/services-fuer-forschende/european-project-center/news/2026-10-13-erasmus-call-2027-restriktionen); [TU Dresden factsheet](https://tu-dresden.de/forschung-transfer/services-fuer-forschende/european-project-center/ressourcen/dateien/dokumente/erasmus-factsheet)).
- **Size:** in 2025, Strand 2 projects received €400,000–800,000 ([University of Montenegro](https://www.ucg.ac.me/skladiste/blog_616807/objava_191607/fajlovi/CBHE%20-%20requirements%20for%20submission%20of%20project%20proposals.pdf)). Strand 3 received €0.6–1 million over 36–48 months in 2026.
- **Coverage:** the curriculum assessment, continuing professional development, the doctoral pathway and partnership building, with Ukrainian universities paid directly.
- **Consortium:** it needs higher-education institutions from at least two EU or associated countries, so a university outside Sweden must join Uppsala and KI.

Projects from this round would start around late 2027.

Because 2027 is the last call year of the current EU programmes, and FP10 money is unlikely before mid-2028, a later start in practice means **applying between now and April 2027 for a start in late 2027 or early 2028**. Pushing the start further, to mid-2028, lands in the gap between the two EU budgets.

**A longer, phased project is also a better story for funders.** It turns one bid into a programme that bridges the December 2027 cliff.

- **Phase 0, now to late 2027 (bridge).** Collect baseline data on the September–December 2026 course cohort now. If the research starts later without it, WS2 loses the before-and-after design that makes it strong. Possible funding: internal Uppsala or KI money, or the evaluation budget of the existing Sida education grant, if its terms allow. Use an SIUCP 2027 grant for partnership formation and to design the doctoral pathway.
- **Phase 1, late 2027 or early 2028 to 2030–31.** Make CBHE the hub for WS3, WS5 and the continuing partnership. Add MSCA Staff Exchanges for the Swedish study exchange and researcher secondments, and a research core of roughly €0.3–0.7 million for WS2 and WS4 from NNF, RJ or OHAMR. Ukrainian partners lead NRFU bids for a Ukrainian-funded strand.
- **Phase 2, 2029 onward (scale-up).** Options: an ICARS project led by the Ministry of Health, EU4Health or FP10, and Sida's successor Ukraine strategy.

**The trade-offs need managing.**

- **Education money is not research money.** CBHE and MSCA fund capacity and mobility, not research as such. The evaluation (WS2) and systems synthesis (WS4) still need a research funder, so keep a small research core.
- **The Ukrainian share changes with the funder.** CBHE and MSCA pay Ukrainian partners directly. OHAMR may not pay them at all, since no Ukrainian funder is confirmed. NRFU pays only the Ukrainian side.
- **Application caps.** In 2026, Erasmus+ allowed at most two CBHE applications per coordinator ([Lovguiden](https://www.lovguiden.dk/en/det-offentlige/eacea-uddannelse-audiovisuel-og-kultur/2025-12-18-erasmus-cbhe-2026-information-om-tilskud-til-videregaaende-uddannelse)). A TU Dresden notice of 13 October 2026 reports a limit of two KA2 applications per organisation in the 2027 call ([TU Dresden](https://tu-dresden.de/forschung-transfer/services-fuer-forschende/european-project-center/news/2026-10-13-erasmus-call-2027-restriktionen)); it is unclear whether this is an EU or an internal rule. Reserve a slot with Uppsala's grants office early.
- **The MSCA cross-sector rule.** Sweden and Ukraine are both inside Horizon Europe as Member State and Associated Country. Secondments between them therefore have to cross sectors: hospital, ZDOROVI or Nursing Association staff go to Uppsala or KI, or Uppsala and KI researchers go to them. An academic-to-academic secondment, such as Bogomolets to KI, would likely not qualify (background knowledge; verify in the 2027 work programme).

**If Sida invites a full proposal,** ask whether budget and duration may differ from the concept note; the call allows SEK 8–14 million over 18–24 months. A slimmer Sida core focused on WS2 and WS4 (around SEK 8–10 million), with WS3 and WS5 moved to CBHE and MSCA, would reduce dependence on one funder and show co-funding. Only make this change if Sida confirms it is allowed (contact: callforsk2026@sida.se, per [SweDev](https://swedev.dev/sida-call-for-concept-notes-health-and-srhr-research-partnerships-in-lmics-and-ukraine/)).

## Conclusion

The funding picture calls for a hub-and-spoke design, not a search for a single replacement funder. Sida remains the only hub big enough for the whole project. Every other source funds one spoke:

- **doctoral mobility:** MSCA, SIUCP and VR;
- **curriculum reform:** Erasmus+ capacity building;
- **hospital practice:** Klinikpartnerschaften and NNF;
- **policy and gender research:** RJ;
- **scale-up:** EU4Health and ICARS.

Almost every spoke needs a lead from a specific country, so choosing partners is choosing funders. The German hospital, Norwegian nursing faculty and international humanitarian NGO the team recruits in the next two months will decide which 2027 calls it can enter. The same applies to the IPC agenda. WHO's new nursing curriculum tool, ICARS's 2026–2030 strategy and the Global Fund's tuberculosis guidance all now name IPC explicitly, yet none of the funders identified has a nursing-specific AMR window. The project's nursing angle is therefore a niche the team must explain to each funder, because no funder is yet asking for it.

The calendar also converges on one cliff. AMR Contain, Norway's health agreement, Sweden's Ukraine strategy and the EU's 2021–2027 programmes all end in or around December 2027, and FP10 and the next Erasmus+ will not pay out before mid-2028. A project running from January 2027 to December 2028 is therefore a bridge across that gap. That is the argument to put to Sida now. It is also why 2027 should be spent building the follow-on rather than chasing small grants: a 2028 OHAMR consortium, a Widening bid led by Ternopil National Medical University or Bogomolets, and an ICARS expression of interest from the Ministry of Health.
