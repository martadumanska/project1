## 2. Question form (Microsoft Forms)

Build it in Microsoft Forms, like the registration form: **New Form**, then paste the text below.

**Form title:** Your questions for Seminar 2 – From Crisis to Contribution

**Form description:**
On 4 November, around 45–50 minutes of the online seminar are set aside for an open discussion with the speakers: Prof. Serhiy Dubrov, Prof. Dorit Nitzan and Natalia Pidmurniak. Help us shape it by sharing the questions you would most like to discuss. We will group them into 2–3 focus questions for the panel. Please respond by Monday, 2 November.

### Questions

**1. What question(s) would you like to raise in the discussion?**
Type: Text, *Long answer*. **Required.**
Subtitle: *You can ask more than one question. Questions to a specific speaker and broader questions are both welcome.*

**2. Who is your question mainly for?**
Type: Choice, *Multiple answers* on. Optional.
- Prof. Serhiy Dubrov – Ukraine from the inside: the health system under pressure
- Prof. Dorit Nitzan – From emergency response to health diplomacy
- Natalia Pidmurniak – A youth perspective on international cooperation
- All speakers / the panel as a whole

**3. The seminar is built around a paradox. Which side would you most like to explore?**
Type: Choice, single answer. Optional.
- Why does a country under daily attack choose to become a security donor?
- How can a country in acute need contribute anything to others at all?
- Both
- My question is about something else

**4. If we select your question, may we mention your name when it is raised?**
Type: Choice, single answer. **Required.**
- Yes, you may mention my name and organisation
- No, please keep my question anonymous

**5. Your name and organisation (optional)**
Type: Text, *Short answer*. Optional.

### Settings (… menu → Settings)

- **Who can fill out this form:** *Anyone can respond*. Participants are outside your organisation, so they must not be asked to sign in.
- **Accept responses:** on. Optionally set an **end date** of 2 November, 23:59.
- **Get email notification of each response:** optional. This is handy if you want to see questions as they arrive.
- When the form is ready, click **Collect responses**, then **Copy link**. Paste that link into the email above.

### After the deadline

Open **Responses** and click **Open results in Excel**. Questions 2 and 3 let you sort by speaker and by side of the paradox, which makes it quick to cluster the submissions into the 2–3 focus questions for Prof. Hulchiy as moderator.
